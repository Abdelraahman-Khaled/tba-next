What to Look for When Buying Coffee?

When selecting the right coffee, keep the following points in mind:

1. Whole Beans vs. Ground Coffee

Coffee is typically available either as whole beans or ground. Whenever possible, we recommend opting for whole beans. True coffee enthusiasts prefer buying whole beans and grinding them at home or at a local café, as this helps preserve the freshness and authentic flavor of the coffee.

2. Freshness of Coffee Beans

When discussing freshness, there are two key dates to look for on the coffee bag: the roasting date and the purchase date. These are distinct; the roasting date indicates how fresh the coffee is and when it was roasted, while the purchase date provides guidance on how long the coffee will remain good, though it doesn’t guarantee the freshness of the brewed cup.

3. Roast Level and Degree

Do you prefer light, fruity beans or dark, chocolatey ones? Consider your personal roast preference before placing an order. Before the coffee reaches your cup, its aroma should be irresistible. Good coffee should never smell unpleasant or harsh; instead, it should have an enticing and strong aroma that captivates the senses.

4. Coffee Flavor

Taste a variety of roasted coffees to explore different flavor profiles. Regardless of the roast level, the best coffees should have a rich and robust taste. Some may be smooth and mild, while others might be more intense and pronounced, but all should deliver a fresh flavor derived from freshly roasted beans.

Top 5 Coffee Varieties for 2025

If you need a caffeine boost in the morning, you know how important it is to prepare the perfect cup of coffee. Whether you use a traditional drip coffee maker, an automatic drip machine, or a French press, discover with TBA the best coffee varieties for 2025:

1. Copper Cow Classic Coffee – Authentic Vietnamese Experience

While Copper Cow Classic coffee is famous for its single-serve brew, our team believes its beans deserve equal attention. This dark, roasted Vietnamese coffee offers a unique balance of deep flavor and full richness without being overly heavy or acidic. Its smooth and harmonious flavors make it an ideal choice that coffee lovers will return to cup after cup.

2. Bunz Coffee Company – Bright Flavor with High Caffeine

You may be used to choosing dark roast when craving caffeine, but light roasts often contain more caffeine per serving. If you seek a distinctive flavor or a brighter coffee than the traditional dark blend, try Bunz Coffee Company’s single-origin Ethiopia. Before sipping, you’ll notice its vibrant, unique aroma, and upon tasting, sharp and acidic notes reveal layers of enjoyment and a rich caffeine boost to energize your morning.

3. Lavazza Classico – Perfectly Balanced Medium Roast

If you’re torn between light and dark roast, enhance your experience with a superb medium roast like Lavazza Classico. This popular choice offers a harmonious balance of flavor, bitterness, and acidity. This equilibrium comes from a carefully roasted blend of Arabica and Robusta beans, resulting in a medium-bodied cup with dried fruit and nutty flavors.

4. Monarch Blend by Onyx Coffee Lab – Espresso Flavor in Every Cup

Did you know regular coffee and espresso come from the same beans? The main difference lies in the roasting method (usually very dark for espresso) and preparation using an espresso machine. If you want espresso flavor without purchasing an espresso machine, or if you prefer a bold dark roast, our team at TBA highly recommends the Monarch Blend by Onyx Coffee Lab. This delicious blend stands out on its own but is also excellent with a splash of milk for an espresso-like experience when brewed in a moka pot.

5. La Colombe Blue – The Ultimate Luxury Organic Coffee

If you’re a fan of organic coffee, you likely want an organic option that truly stands out. While several organic brands perform well in tests, coffee connoisseurs rank La Colombe Blue among the best. This dark roast combines beans from Honduras, Nicaragua, and Peru, producing a richly flavored cup with a sharp note at the first sip. It may be too intense for light roast lovers but is the perfect choice if you seek a strong, dark organic coffee.
