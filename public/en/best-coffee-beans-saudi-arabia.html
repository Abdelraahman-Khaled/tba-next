Types of Coffee Beans Popular in Saudi Arabia

In Saudi Arabia, coffee culture is thriving, and consumers are increasingly seeking a variety of coffee beans to satisfy different tastes and brewing styles. The most popular types include:

  • Arabica Beans – Known for their smooth flavor, mild acidity, and aromatic profile, Arabica beans are highly favored in specialty cafés and homes alike.
  • Robusta Beans – Rich in caffeine and with a stronger, bolder taste, Robusta beans are ideal for espresso blends and consumers who prefer an intense coffee experience.
  • Single-Origin Beans – Sourced from specific regions around the world, these beans offer unique flavors and are perfect for coffee connoisseurs looking for distinctive tasting notes.
  • Blended Coffee Beans – Combining different varieties to achieve balanced flavor, aroma, and body, blends are popular for both commercial and household use.
  • Locally Roasted Beans – Increasingly popular in Saudi Arabia, these beans provide freshness and a personalized roasting profile that appeals to modern coffee enthusiasts.
