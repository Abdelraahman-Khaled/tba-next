Top Exported Coffee Types in 2025

The global coffee export market demonstrated notable resilience in 2025, with total exports reaching 13 million bags in March alone, slightly up from 12.92 million in March 2024. According to the latest data from the International Coffee Organization (ICO), the market is increasingly dynamic, influenced by price fluctuations and evolving consumer preferences.

Here are the top coffee-exporting countries of 2025:

1. Brazil – The World’s Leading Coffee Exporter

Brazil produces approximately 70% Arabica and 30% Robusta beans. Brazilian Arabica is prized for its mild sweetness, chocolate and nutty notes, low acidity, and medium body. Meanwhile, Robusta (locally known as Conilon) is gaining recognition and is widely used in espresso blends and instant coffee. Brazil contributes about 70% of the world’s total coffee exports.

2. Vietnam – The Robusta Powerhouse

Robusta dominates Vietnam’s production at around 95%, known for its high caffeine content and bold, earthy flavor. Vietnam, the second-largest coffee producer globally, continues improving Robusta quality while gradually increasing Arabica cultivation in highland areas.

3. Colombia – Premium Arabica Producer

Colombia’s mountainous terrain offers the perfect environment for high-quality Arabica coffee, including varieties like Castillo, Caturra, and the elite Geisha. Colombian coffee is known for its medium to high acidity, full body, and distinct caramel, citrus, and chocolate notes.

4. India – A Unique Flavor Profile

India produces about 70% Robusta and 30% Arabica, offering distinctive varieties such as Monsooned Malabar and Mysore Nuggets. Indian coffee is often grown under shade alongside spices like cardamom and pepper, creating complex and unique flavor profiles not found elsewhere.

Choosing the Right Source for Coffee Import and Distribution

Importing and distributing coffee is not just about delivering a product it's about delivering a complete experience. The ideal supplier goes beyond sourcing beans; they ensure on-time delivery, provide the necessary brewing equipment, offer technical training, and guide you in selecting the perfect blends for your menu.

Whether you run a premium café, a fine-dining restaurant, or an artisanal bakery, the quality of your coffee directly impacts your brand reputation and customer satisfaction.

What do professional coffee suppliers offer?

  • Sourcing: Selecting beans from top coffee-growing regions worldwide, often working directly with farmers and cooperatives to guarantee consistency and variety.
  • Roasting: Precision roasting in-house, carefully adjusting time, temperature, and roasting profiles to highlight each bean’s unique flavor.
  • Equipment: Providing state-of-the-art brewing equipment — from espresso machines to grinders and filters — along with maintenance and repair services to keep operations smooth.
  • Training: Offering staff training to help your team master the art of brewing, using the supplier’s products and equipment effectively.

Questions to Ask Potential Coffee Suppliers

Before committing to a coffee supplier, asking the right questions can uncover valuable insights and ensure you're making an informed decision. Whether you’re looking to import and distribute premium coffee, consider the following:

Where does your coffee come from?

Understanding the origin of the beans offers insight into sourcing practices and unique flavor profiles.

  • What is your roasting process?

    Ask about roasting techniques, duration, and how they ensure consistent flavor across batches.

  • Do you offer organic or Fair Trade options?

    If ethical sourcing is a priority for your business, ensure the supplier can meet these expectations.

  • What equipment do you provide?

    Determine whether they can supply and maintain brewing equipment like espresso machines, grinders, and filters.

  • What kind of training do you offer?

    Inquire about staff training and support services that help ensure consistent coffee quality in your establishment.

  • What is your delivery schedule?

    Reliable, timely deliveries are crucial. Ask about distributor locations, logistics, and their delivery timeline.
